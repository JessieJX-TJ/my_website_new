01

Overview

What it is

This project explores how AI can support the construction and organization of structured knowledge from unstructured documents. Using automotive research documents as an example, the system allows users to upload source materials, configure extraction skills, and generate an ontology containing concepts and relationships identified from the text.

What I did

I designed the interaction across three connected parts: Ontology, Skill, and Graph.

  • The Ontology section supports document processing, AI-generated knowledge structuring, and human review.
  • Skills define how the AI interprets and extracts information, allowing users to create, edit, upload, and reuse different extraction configurations.
  • The Graph section visualizes the resulting ontology as nodes and relationships, while allowing users to inspect and adjust existing knowledge structures.

Core outcomes

The final interface establishes a workflow from unstructured documents to AI-generated knowledge, human refinement, and visual exploration, making the underlying structure of the extracted information accessible through the interface.

02

Problem Space

Making AI-generated knowledge understandable

Structured concepts are not automatically meaningful. Users need to see where they come from, spot gaps, and refine the result.

Configuring how AI interprets information

Extraction cannot stay fixed. Users need to see the active Skill and create, edit, or reuse it for different documents.

Working with complex relationships

Dense graphs are hard to follow. Users need to move between the whole structure and one concept without losing their place.

Connecting generation with human review

Generated knowledge is a draft. The interface needs a path to review, edit, and validate it before it becomes the knowledge base.

03

Design Response

Turn AI extraction into an editable workflow

Instead of presenting AI-generated ontology as a final output, I designed the workflow around generation and refinement. Users can upload source documents, select or configure a Skill, review the generated structure, and make corrections before submitting it for further use.

Make extraction logic configurable through Skills

Skills act as configurable instructions for how the AI extracts and organizes information. I designed a consistent interaction pattern for creating, editing, uploading, and reusing Skills, allowing users to adapt the extraction process to different types of source material.

Connect structured data with visual exploration

I designed the Graph as a visual representation of the ontology rather than an independent drawing tool. Nodes and relationships are generated from the structured ontology, while users can inspect and edit existing elements through the graph and corresponding information cards.

Coordinate graph and list views

To help users work with dense knowledge structures, I connected the graph with a structured list view. Selecting a node or relationship in the list locates it in the graph and visually isolates the selected element, allowing users to move between overview and detail without losing context.

04

Reflection

The project helped me explore how AI-generated knowledge can be made inspectable and editable through interaction. However, the current design focuses more on structuring and refining AI outputs than on helping users understand why particular concepts and relationships were generated. Future iterations could therefore explore how interfaces can better support users in evaluating the provenance and reliability of AI-generated knowledge.
