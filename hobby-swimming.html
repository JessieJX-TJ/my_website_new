Hobby

Swimming

I love swimming — it clears my head and keeps me moving when screens and schedules get loud.

In the water, rhythm matters more than noise. Laps help me reset attention and return to design work with a steadier mind.

It’s a simple habit, but a reliable one: body first, then ideas — the same order I try to protect when projects get complex.
