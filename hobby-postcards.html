Hobby

Drawing Postcards

I draw postcards and send them to my friends — a small ritual of noticing, making, and staying close across distance.

Each card starts with a quick sketch or a quiet scene from daily life. I like how a postcard has to say enough in a limited space — a little like designing an interface: clear, warm, and personal.

Sending them keeps creativity playful and social. It’s not about perfect illustration; it’s about showing up for people with something handmade.
