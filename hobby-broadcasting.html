Hobby

Broadcasting

I was the Sports Announcer at the 2023 China Tennis Open — guiding the crowd through pace, drama, and decisive moments.

Live announcing taught me timing, clarity, and presence: how to hold attention without overexplaining, and how energy can shape an experience.

Those skills travel into design — especially when communicating product stories, workshop narratives, or moments that need to feel alive.
