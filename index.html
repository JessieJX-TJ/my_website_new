About Me

Hi there, I'm Jessie!

UI/UX Designer · Design Researcher

I’m Xi (Jessie) Ji, a UI/UX designer and design researcher interested in how people interact and work with increasingly capable AI systems.

I design AI-powered products and study emerging interactions between people and AI, particularly in creative and professional practice. My work explores how AI can participate in human activities while remaining understandable, steerable, and responsive to the contexts in which people work and create.

Design Philosophy

How I view design and what I pursue

  • I see design as a way of shaping how people encounter and act in the world.

    Design is not only about producing artifacts or solving predefined problems. It shapes the relationships between people, technologies, information, and the situations in which they act. I am interested in design as a way of questioning these relationships and imagining how they might be different.

  • AI is making these relationships increasingly dynamic.

    As AI moves beyond being a tool that executes predefined instructions, it can interpret, generate, adapt, and increasingly participate in human activities. This is changing not only what we can create, but also the boundaries between author and tool, intention and execution, and human and machine agency. Recent design research increasingly frames AI as a material, collaborator, and participant rather than simply an instrument for automation.

  • I want to explore what new forms of human–AI relationships could be.

    I am interested in designing interactions that allow people and AI to work together without reducing either side to a fixed role. Through design research, I explore emerging practices, develop and prototype alternative interactions, and study how these interactions shape people's understanding, decisions, and ways of acting. Ultimately, I want to contribute to human–AI relationships that are not only more capable, but also more meaningful, understandable, and responsive to the contexts in which people live and work.
