01

Overview

What it is

Regulatory work often involves navigating large amounts of technical and procedural information while completing structured tasks. I worked on an AI agent that rethought this workflow around dialogue, allowing users to interact with the system while progressing through regulatory-related tasks.

What I did

As the UX/UI designer, I designed the interaction flows and interface presentation of the agent. Rather than reproducing conventional enterprise interfaces based on forms, tables, and separate functions, I explored how dialogue could become part of the primary workspace. The agent could guide users through a task, provide relevant information at the appropriate point, and remind or correct users when they made mistakes or misunderstood the process.

Core outcomes

The project resulted in a dialogue-based interface that integrated AI assistance directly into the workflow, shifting the interaction from navigating a set of tools toward working through a task with an active AI agent.

02

Problem Space

From tool navigation to task-oriented interaction

Traditional enterprise interfaces often organize complex workflows around menus, forms, tables, and separate functions. Users need to understand the structure of the system before they can complete a task. The challenge was to make AI assistance part of the task itself rather than another function users had to navigate to.

Making AI assistance actionable

Providing information is not enough when users are working through a procedural task. The system needs to surface relevant information at the right moment and help users understand what to do next, without interrupting the workflow.

Supporting users when they go off course

Users may make mistakes, misunderstand requirements, or provide incomplete information during a task. The interface therefore needs to support AI interventions that can remind, guide, or correct users while keeping the interaction understandable and controllable.

03

Design Response

3.1

Make dialogue part of the workflow

I reorganized the workflow around ongoing dialogue rather than treating it as an additional feature. Users can communicate their needs, receive guidance, and progressively complete tasks within the same interaction space, while structured interface elements remain available where needed.

3.2

Provide guidance at the point of action

Instead of requiring users to actively search for help, the agent can surface relevant information and guidance as the task progresses. Dialogue becomes a way for the system to respond to the user's current situation and support the next step.

3.3

Use dialogue to handle errors and misunderstandings

When users provide incorrect or incomplete information, the agent can respond through dialogue rather than simply returning an error state. It can explain the issue, remind users of relevant requirements, and guide them toward an appropriate next step.

3.4

04

Reflection

The project showed me how conversational interaction can turn AI capabilities into active support within an existing enterprise workflow. At the same time, making an agent more proactive also raises questions about how much initiative it should take, when an intervention is helpful, and how users can remain aware of and in control of the process.
